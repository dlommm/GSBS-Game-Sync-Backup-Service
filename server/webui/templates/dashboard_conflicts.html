How resolution works

  • Keep server version (here) dismisses the conflict — the server's copy stands, and the device will pick it up according to its conflict policy.
  • Keep the device's copy instead: on that device, open the tray → ⚠ Resolve conflicts → Keep all local files. Its push then supersedes this conflict automatically.
  • Rescue any version: nothing is deleted — open the file's version history to download or restore an older copy.
  • Prevent repeats: set a per-game conflict policy (last-write-wins, keep local, keep server) in the client's Settings on each device.
