Help

What each status on this PC means, and what to try when something looks wrong.

Statuses

Connection
Whether this PC is signed in to your GSBS server. "Re-login required" means its token was revoked; open Setup and sign in again.
Last sync
When the latest upload and download cycle finished, and whether it worked.
Watcher
Notices save changes the moment they happen. "Recovering" fixes itself; syncing carries on at the interval meanwhile.
Pending uploads
Saves queued while the server was out of reach. They retry on their own for 7 days.
Conflicts
The same save changed on two PCs. Your conflict policy picked a copy; the details are in Insights.

Something wrong?

  1. Not connected. Open Setup and sign in with your server address and account.
  2. A game isn't syncing. Check it's listed under Status; if not, add its save folder.
  3. Sync errors. Open Logs and filter to errors.
  4. A save looks wrong. Load an older version from the server's Library, on that game's version history.
  5. Still stuck. Open the log file and attach it to a GitHub issue.
